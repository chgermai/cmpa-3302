## Overview
HACS provides Home Assistant with community-built integrations, dashboards, and themes. It is considered an add-on to the platform.
### What I Use It For
- Installing community integrations for devices that don't have official support.
- Custom dashboard cards for a cleaner look.
### Lesson Learned
HACS is not an add-on you can leave untouched for years and expect to keep working. As part of the [[../06-guides/rebuilding-home-assistant|rebuild]], I reinstalled HACS fresh and didn't even attempt to migrate any of the configs.
